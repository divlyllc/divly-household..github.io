Delete your Divly account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Divly account (the Divly app by Divly LLC) and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

From inside the app

  1. Open Divly and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Account settings and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is immediately scheduled for deletion.

By email

If you can't access the app, email hello@divly.co from the email address on your Divly account and ask us to delete your account. We'll verify and process the request.

What gets deleted

When your request is processed, we permanently delete your account and the personal data associated with it, including:

  • Your profile (name, email, photo, preferences)
  • Your tasks, habits, shopping lists and list items
  • Your household data, check-ins, and notification settings
  • Your Ask Divly conversation history

Timing and what we keep

Deletion includes a 30-day grace period: for 30 days after your request, your data is retained so you can restore the account by signing back in. After 30 days, the data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

After permanent deletion we retain only the minimum records required by law or for legitimate business needs, such as records needed for tax and accounting, and purchase/transaction records held by the app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) for any subscriptions. We do not keep your personal content beyond that.

Questions? Contact hello@divly.co. See also our Privacy Policy.
